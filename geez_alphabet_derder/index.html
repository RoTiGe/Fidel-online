🎯 Geez Alphabet Derder

📱 Mobile Controls:

👆 Tap and drag letters to drop zones

🖱️ Desktop Controls:

🖱️ Click and drag letters to drop zones

How to Play:

  • 🎯 Letters appear randomly at the top
  • 📝 Place them in the correct order
  • ✅ Green slots = correct, ❌ Red slots = wrong
  • 📢 Each letter pronounces when placed
  • 🎉 Complete 3 words per stage to advance

Difficulty Levels:

  • Stage 1 Learning - 2-3 letter words
  • Stage 2 Practice - 3-4 letter words
  • Stage 3 Master - 4-5 letter words
  • Stage 4 Expert - 5-6 letter words
  • Stage 5 Champion - 6+ letter words

Features:

  • 🔄 Undo button to rearrange letters
  • ⏱ Optional timer for timed challenges
  • 📊 Track your progress and accuracy
  • 🎓 Learn letter sequences and word formation

💡 Tip: You can click on placed letters to remove and rearrange them!
