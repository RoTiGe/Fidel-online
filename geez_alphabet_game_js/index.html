📚 Geez Alphabet Learning Game

📱 Mobile Controls:

🎮 Touch and drag to move your collection circle

🖱️ Desktop Controls:

🎮 Move your mouse to control the collection circle

How to Play:

  • 🎯 Collect letters in the correct order to spell words
  • 📢 Hear the pronunciation of each letter
  • ⭐ Earn points for correct sequences

Difficulty Levels:

Stage 1 Learning - Letters fall slowly. Collect in order = 1 pt. Out of order = 0.5 pts.

Stage 2 Practice - Letters fall faster. Same scoring.

Stage 3 Master - Must collect in correct order! Wrong order = -0.5 pts!

Stage 4 Multi-Direction - Letters come from top, left, AND right!

Stage 5 Diagonal Chaos - Letters fly diagonally from all edges! 🚀

⚠️ Complete 3 words per stage to advance. Score 0 in Stage 1 = Game Over!
