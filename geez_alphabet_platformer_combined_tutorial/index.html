💡 Tutorial Mode - Guided Learning

Welcome to the Guided Tutorial!

Perfect for beginners! The next letter to collect will glow golden and pulse!

📱 Mobile/Tablet Controls:

  • 👆 Tap on-screen arrow buttons (◄ ►) to move left/right
  • ⬆️ Tap JUMP button to jump

⌨️ Desktop/Laptop Controls:

  • 🏃 Arrow Keys (← →) or A/D to move left/right
  • ⬆️ Space or ↑ to jump

How to Play:

  • 🎯 Follow the glowing letters to spell words
  • 📚 Listen to each letter's pronunciation
  • 🚪 Enter the portal after collecting all letters

Tutorial Features:

  • ✨ Glowing & pulsing hints for next letter
  • 📍 Progress checkpoints at each stage
  • 🎓 Beginner-friendly difficulty
  • ⚠️ Enemies spawn after 30 seconds

💡 Tip: The glowing effect makes it easy to know which letter to collect next!
